I am an applied mathematician from Auckland, New Zealand. My recent research during my masters developed methods to approximate the risk of tipping in tipping-point systems. That work sits at the intersection of stochastic analysis and real-world modelling. Consequently I have also recently become drawn towards modelling the effects of variable renewable energy in electricity networks/markets. I'm particularly interested in applying my stochastic modelling knowledge to tackle the challenges (and opportunities) that the uncertainty and variability of renewables present.

To see some of my recent work, please have a look at my Projects section. Outside of mathematics, I hold a wide range of interests and hobbies. I am a keen cook and love sports and the outdoors. See my Diversions section for a few deep dives into my non-academic interests.
